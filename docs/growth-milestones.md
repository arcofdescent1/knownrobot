# KnownRobot growth milestones

KnownRobot's long-term goal is to become the trusted discovery, distribution and evidence network for robotics: the place practitioners use to find robot policies and datasets, understand whether they fit a specific embodiment, inspect evaluation evidence, publish reproducible results and build professional contribution history.

This is analogous to Hugging Face's role in machine learning distribution, but KnownRobot's initial advantage is narrower: connecting immutable artifacts to hardware configurations, protocols, measured outcomes, failures and independent review.

## Operating rules

1. A milestone is complete only when every exit criterion has evidence.
2. Pages published, messages sent, issues opened, impressions and followers are activities or leading indicators—not milestone outcomes.
3. Unknown measurements remain unknown. Agents may not replace missing analytics with estimates.
4. A qualified visit reaches a substantive artifact, compatibility, validator, assessment or evaluation surface and produces meaningful engagement.
5. A canonical artifact is bound to an immutable source revision with attribution and a stable KnownRobot identity.
6. An evidence record preserves its execution and trust boundaries; metadata validation is never counted as measured evaluation.
7. An active contributor completes at least one meaningful public action during the stated measurement window. A repeat contributor completes a second meaningful action on a different date.
8. The milestone dates are targets, not permission to waive the exit criteria.

## Milestone 0 — Measurable and discoverable

Target: first 30 days.

Exit criteria:

- Read-only Google Search Console and product analytics are available to the agents or their daily evidence source.
- Conversion events cover validator or assessment engagement, artifact submission, practitioner conversation and sprint application.
- At least 90% of strategic indexable URLs are indexed.
- The sitemap is accepted and no material crawl, canonical or redirect defect remains open.
- A baseline ledger exists for target queries, qualified sessions, workflow starts, conversions, referring domains, prospects and outreach.
- Public trust boundaries and conversion paths are understandable without founder explanation.

SEO owns indexation, query baselines and organic measurement. Growth owns conversion-path clarity, prospect tracking and intake measurement.

## Milestone 1 — First committed cohort

Target: 90 days.

Exit criteria:

- 100 qualified organic sessions in a rolling 30-day window.
- 10 non-branded target queries produce search impressions.
- 5 organic workflow starts.
- 25 completed practitioner conversations.
- 5 qualified design partners committed to a concrete action.
- 10 attributable public artifact submissions.
- 5 published assessments or evidence records.
- 3 relevant earned referring domains.
- 3 contributors complete a second meaningful action.
- At least one practitioner conversation or artifact submission is attributable to organic search.

## Milestone 2 — Evidence liquidity

Target: six months.

Exit criteria:

- 500 qualified organic sessions per rolling 30 days.
- 25 maintained high-intent queries rank in the top 20.
- 10 organic conversions per month.
- 50 discoverable canonical policy, dataset, hardware or evaluation artifacts.
- 25 attributable assessment or evaluation records.
- 10 independent contributing teams.
- 10 repeat contributors.
- One completed multi-team reproduction loop.
- Artifact or evaluation intake occurs every month.
- 10 relevant projects or communities link to, cite or integrate KnownRobot.

## Milestone 3 — Recurring network loop

Target: 12 months.

Exit criteria:

- 2,000 qualified organic sessions per rolling 30 days.
- 50 maintained high-intent queries rank in the top 10.
- 30 organic conversions per month.
- 250 canonical artifacts.
- 100 evidence records, including failed and blocked attempts.
- 30 active contributors in a rolling 90-day window.
- 5 independent reproductions per month.
- A monthly reproduction sprint includes at least 3 participating teams.
- At least 10 maintainers or organizations link, export or integrate.
- Organic discovery contributes evidence from at least 10 independent teams.

## Milestone 4 — Default ecosystem destination

Target: 24–36 months.

Exit criteria:

- 10,000 qualified organic sessions per rolling 30 days.
- The majority of the maintained high-intent query set ranks in the top 10.
- 100 organic conversions per month.
- 1,000 canonical artifacts.
- 500 measured or reviewed evidence records.
- 100 active contributor organizations.
- 25 external integrations, badges or machine-readable exports.
- At least 25 authoritative robotics projects, labs, vendors or publications cite or link to KnownRobot.
- Contribution and discovery loops continue without founder-led outreach.

## Agent ownership

### SEO agent

The SEO agent owns qualified organic acquisition: indexation, non-branded query coverage, rankings, qualified organic sessions, workflow starts attributable to search, organic conversions and earned referring domains. Its daily work must advance the current milestone's tightest SEO constraint.

### Growth agent

The Growth agent owns the human and evidence funnel: practitioner conversations, replies, artifact supply, assessment permission, published records, independent review, repeat contribution, introductions, design partners, sprints and integrations. It tracks every public opportunity through identified, contacted, replied, artifact supplied, assessment drafted, contributor approved, published, independently reviewed and repeat contribution.

### Product

Product work is justified when repeated search or practitioner evidence shows that a missing workflow blocks discovery, contribution, trust or reuse. Neither agent may infer product demand from polite interest alone.

## Review cadence

- Daily: record material movement, the tightest constraint and one next action.
- Weekly: review the milestone scoreboard and stop tactics that produced activity without funnel movement.
- Monthly: verify evidence for every metric, update the target-query and prospect sets, and decide whether the current milestone remains the correct constraint.
- At every milestone: archive the evidence snapshot, record what caused the transition and establish the next milestone baseline before expanding scope.
