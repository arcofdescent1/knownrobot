# Reproduction sprint joint report

Use the exact sprint ID, pinned policy revision and agreed protocol digest from the operating record in the report title/introduction. State actual run and session dates. Distinguish physical hardware from simulation and accurate blocked outcomes from independent reproduction. This is not safety certification.

## Scope and protocol

Link the immutable policy, pre-trial configurations and frozen protocol. Describe the task, reset, success predicate, timeout and planned trial count. Explain the question the cohort tested and the claims this study cannot support. Do not pool configurations or quietly change the denominator.

## Compatibility lessons and improvements

Describe the repeated failures, what matched and what did not, including calibration, observation/action contracts, dependency versions and control rate. For each decisive failure, link the resulting product or adapter issue with a named accountable owner and acceptance test. Distinguish implemented fixes from proposed work; the existence of an issue is not a completed improvement.

## Public results session

Link immutable session notes and any public recording the participants permitted. Preserve corrections, disagreements and limits of interpretation. State who participated rather than treating the public invitation as attendance.

## Portable credit

Credit every team lead, evaluator, reviewer and useful adapter contributor by public handle/name with source links and contribution descriptions. Get permission before publishing affiliations. Cite the policy/dataset authors accurately; selection does not imply their endorsement.

## Corrections and limitations

Link a public correction issue and the operating-record history. Log dated substantive corrections and their evidence/review references. Retain the old conclusion and explain why it changed. Describe sample size, incomplete hardware coverage, missing metadata, safety stops and selection bias.

## Publication record

Publish the report as a full commit-pinned GitHub blob or Hugging Face file. The event lead records that URL in the operating record, obtains the REPORT token binding the report and all evidence/reviews, posts it personally in a public issue comment and records its comment/digest in a reviewed PR. An unconfirmed draft is not a completed sprint.
