Hi @vishal-naveen — I’m working on KnownRobot, an open registry for robot-policy compatibility and reproducibility evidence. TactileVLA-Edge looks like an unusually useful public case because the repository identifies the SO-101 setup, LeRobot/ACT and SmolVLA workflows, a 160-episode dataset, held-out-cell evaluation, videos, limitations, and reported results.

Would you be comfortable with us drafting a metadata-only, attributable KnownRobot assessment pinned to an exact commit? The draft would:

- credit this repository and its authors as the source;
- preserve the Apache-2.0/CERN-OHL-P-2.0 licensing distinctions stated upstream;
- label ACT 18/20 and SmolVLA 16/20 strictly as upstream-reported claims, not KnownRobot measurements;
- state that KnownRobot has not executed the policies or independently verified hardware results;
- publish the validator’s missing-information report and immutable source/file hashes; and
- give you a chance to correct the draft before it is presented as maintainer-reviewed.

The record would not imply endorsement, ownership, certification, or compatibility beyond the evidence in this repository. If you are open to it, a simple “yes” here is enough for us to prepare the draft; we will return with the exact record for review before applying any maintainer-reviewed label. If you would rather not, we will not proceed.
